## Everyone's cup of specialty.

Coffee Planet began in the UAE in 2005 with a simple idea: specialty coffee should be for everyone. Our cafes in Lahore and Mandi Bahauddin serve espresso drinks, iced coffees, teas, sandwiches and sweets in relaxed spaces made for long conversations.
